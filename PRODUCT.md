# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teachers and academic staff in Indonesian schools. They process student academic records — grades, attendance, transcripts — in an office or staff room, often on a local machine with no internet dependency. The primary task is data entry and retrieval: finding a student record, updating it, generating a report. Speed and accuracy matter more than visual polish.

## Product Purpose

SIAAS (Sistem Informasi Administrasi Akademik Siswa) replaces the traditional physical record book (Buku Induk) with an automated digital workflow. It exists to make school administration faster, more accurate, and less error-prone — transforming paper-based student record-keeping into a structured, searchable, offline digital system. Success means an admin staff member can complete their daily record-keeping tasks in fewer steps and with fewer errors than with paper.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, Precise, Authoritative

## Anti-references

- **No consumer-cute aesthetics.** No bubbly buttons, pastel gradients, heavy shadows, or decorative illustrations. The interface communicates institutional trust, not playfulness.
- **No SaaS/cloud gimmicks.** No cloud-sync icons, spinning network indicators, or connectivity prompts. The app is offline-native; the UI should never imply otherwise.
- **No hidden navigation.** Administrative functions are not buried behind gamified menus or multi-layer navigation. Every task takes the minimum number of clicks.
- **No decorative typography.** Inter is the face for a reason — legibility in data-dense screens. No script, display, or "artsy" fonts.
- **No informal copywriting.** No "Hey there!" or "Oopsie!" The tone is clinical, direct, and institutionally formal — this handles official student records.
- **No inconsistent semantic color.** Red, green, and amber are reserved for meaningful status signals (alerts, attendance, approval state). Never decorative.

## Design Principles

1. **Content-first, decoration-last.** Information density and clarity beat ornament. Every pixel serves a purpose.
2. **Minimum interaction cost.** Administrative tasks demand efficiency. Every flow should require the fewest possible clicks to complete.
3. **Institutional trust through precision.** Formal, consistent, predictable. Semantic meaning is never sacrificed for aesthetics.
4. **Offline-native reality.** Built for local operation. No cloud metaphors, no network-dependent patterns.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA is the floor. Every decision starts from legibility and contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Considerations for reduced motion, color blindness (semantic colors must carry secondary indicators like icons or labels, not rely on color alone), and keyboard navigation throughout.
